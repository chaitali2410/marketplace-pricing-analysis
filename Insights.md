# Key Insights – Marketplace Pricing Analysis

## Inventory Analysis
- Kurta is the dominant product category with 801 SKUs, representing the largest share of inventory.
- Kurta Set follows with 342 SKUs, indicating strong focus on coordinated apparel offerings.

## Marketplace Pricing Analysis
- Amazon has the highest average listed price at ₹2237.22 across analyzed marketplaces.
- Myntra has the lowest average listed price at ₹2218.19, suggesting a comparatively aggressive pricing strategy.
- Overall pricing across marketplaces remains closely aligned, with minor average variation.

## Product Pricing Insights
- The highest priced products belong primarily to the Kurta Set category, with top SKUs priced at ₹5997.
- Premium Kurta Set products occupy the upper pricing tier of the catalog.

## Margin Analysis
- Gown category has the highest average margin at ₹2400, making it the most profitable category per unit.
- Kurta Set category generates the second highest average margin at ₹1940.81.
- Tops have the lowest average margin at ₹1339.44.

## Marketplace Price Consistency
- Certain SKUs show price gaps of up to ₹800 across Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra.
- Significant marketplace pricing discrepancies indicate inconsistent cross-platform pricing strategy.

## Product Weight Analysis
- Gown and Kurta Set categories have the highest average weight (0.4 kg).
- Tops are the lightest category with an average weight of 0.2 kg.
